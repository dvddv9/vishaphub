# Data Processing Agreement

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of the agreement between the
Customer and Vishap for the use of the Vishap service and applies to the
processing of Personal Data that Vishap carries out on the Customer's behalf. It
supplements, and does not replace, the [Terms of Service](./TERMS.md) and the

## 1. Definitions

1.1. **"Controller"** means the entity that determines the purposes and means of
the processing of Personal Data — for the purposes of this DPA, the Customer.

1.2. **"Processor"** means the entity that processes Personal Data on behalf of
the Controller — for the purposes of this DPA, Vishap.

1.3. **"Data Subject"** means an identified or identifiable natural person to
whom Personal Data relates.

1.4. **"Personal Data"** means any information relating to a Data Subject as
defined in Article 4(1) GDPR, contained in the material the Customer submits for
analysis or generated by the Service.

1.5. **"Processing"** means any operation performed on Personal Data, including
collection, storage, analysis, transmission and deletion.

1.6. **"Sub-processor"** means a third party engaged by Vishap to process
Personal Data on the Customer's behalf (Section 4).

1.7. **"Customer Data"** means the material submitted for analysis, the generated
reports, and associated metadata.

1.8. **"SCCs"** means the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by European
Commission Decision 2021/914.

1.9. **"Services"** means the Vishap web application, API and command-line
interface described in the Terms of Service.

3.1. **Instructions.** Vishap processes Personal Data only on documented

3.6. **DPIA and prior consultation.** Vishap provides reasonable assistance where

3.7. **Deletion or return on termination.** At the Customer's choice, Vishap

3.8. **Demonstrating compliance.** Vishap makes available the information

## 6. Security Measures

6.1. **Encryption at rest.** Sensitive stored values — API credentials and
integration secrets — are encrypted with Fernet (AES-128-CBC with HMAC-SHA256
authentication).

6.2. **Webhook authentication.** Inbound GitHub webhooks are verified
with HMAC SHA-256 signatures and a timestamp tolerance to prevent replay.

6.3. **Authentication and access control.** Sessions are signed JWTs issued by
Clerk and validated on every request against published keys. The analysis API is
protected by API keys and rate limiting.

6.4. **Multi-tenant isolation.** Job records are isolated with PostgreSQL
row-level security: each request binds a tenant, and policies restrict SELECT,
INSERT, UPDATE and DELETE to rows belonging to that tenant.

6.5. **Audit trail.** SHA-256 hashes of the analysed material make reports
tamper-evident without requiring retention of raw content.

6.6. **Observability.** OpenTelemetry traces and Prometheus metrics record job
identifiers and timings; they do **not** contain the analysed material or file
names.

6.7. **Transport encryption.** All client traffic is served over TLS.

6.8. **Personnel and processes.** Access is granted on a least-privilege basis,
reviewed periodically; a documented incident-response procedure is maintained and
tested.

## 9. Audit and Inspection

9.1. **Audit right.** Once per calendar year, on at least **30 days'** written
notice, the Customer (or an independent auditor bound by confidentiality) may
audit Vishap's compliance with this DPA.

9.2. **Scope and method.** Audits are conducted remotely, during normal business
hours, by reviewing documentation, security questionnaires and, where necessary,
virtual walk-throughs. On-site inspections require mutual written agreement.

9.3. **Costs.** Each party bears its own costs. If the audit reveals a material
breach of this DPA, Vishap reimburses the Customer's reasonable audit costs.

9.4. **Limits.** The Customer may not audit on-site more than once per year,
access the data of other customers, or perform penetration testing without prior
written consent.

9.5. **Alternative.** Vishap may satisfy this Section by providing a current
independent security certification or a completed industry-standard questionnaire
(ISO 27001, SOC 2 or equivalent), where available.

10.4. **Exclusions.** Neither party is liable under this DPA for losses caused by
