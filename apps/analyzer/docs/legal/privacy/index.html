Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 18, 2026 Data controller: dvddv9 (Individual Entrepreneur) ("Vishap", "we", "us") Contact: dvddvd9h@outlook.com

This Privacy Policy explains what personal data we collect, why we collect it, how long we keep it, and the rights you have. It covers the Vishap web application, API and command-line interface.

1. What We Collect​

Account data (via Clerk). Email address, name (if you provide it), profile image URL, organisation membership, and authentication metadata. We do not store passwords — authentication is handled by Clerk.

Material you submit for analysis. The code, configuration, specifications, logs or data you upload or paste, together with the filename you provide. This material may contain personal data; see Section 3.3.

Derived analysis data. The generated report, and an audit trail containing SHA-256 hashes of the analysed material (material_hash, material_hash_full), PII-detection flags, compliance mappings, a provenance record (for example the git commit analysed), token counts and cost.

Usage and technical data. IP address, request paths and timings, HTTP status codes, job counts and durations, and error diagnostics. Collected through Prometheus metrics, structured application logs, and OpenTelemetry traces (exported to Tempo and visualised in Grafana).

Billing data (via Stripe). Customer identifier, subscription status, billing period, metered usage and invoice status. Card details are entered directly into Stripe and never reach our servers.

Integration data (GitHub App). Installation identifiers, repository names, webhook payloads and the commit references we analyse.

2. How We Use Your Data​

  • Provide the Service — run the six-stage analysis pipeline and return the report to you.
  • Authenticate and secure your account — verify identity, enforce access control, detect abuse and rate-limit requests.
  • Bill you — manage subscriptions, meter usage and process payments.
  • Operate and improve the Service — measure performance, diagnose failures, and understand which features are used.
  • Communicate — respond to support requests and send service notices. We do not send marketing email without consent.
  • Comply with law — meet legal, tax and accounting obligations, and respond to lawful requests.

Legal bases (GDPR). Performance of a contract (Articles 6(1)(b)) for providing the Service and billing; legitimate interests (6(1)(f)) for security, abuse prevention and service reliability; consent (6(1)(a)) where we ask for it; and legal obligation (6(1)(c)) for tax and accounting records.

Automated decision-making. The Service produces automated risk assessments of the material you submit. These assess code and configuration, not individuals, and do not produce legal effects concerning you. Where a report concerns personal data, human review remains your responsibility.

3.5. No sale of personal data. We do not sell personal data and do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

5. Your Rights (GDPR)​

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another jurisdiction with comparable law, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Rectification — correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure — request deletion ("right to be forgotten"), subject to legal retention obligations.
  • Restriction — ask us to limit processing while a concern is investigated.
  • Portability — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Objection — object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Withdraw consent — at any time, where processing is based on consent.
  • Complain — lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

5.1. How to exercise these rights. Email dvddvd9h@outlook.com from the address associated with your account. We respond within 30 days and may ask you to verify your identity.

5.2. Self-service (planned). A programmatic endpoint for data export and deletion — GET /api/me/data and DELETE /api/me/data — is not yet implemented (TODO). Until it ships, requests are handled manually by email.

5.3. International transfers. Some sub-processors operate outside your jurisdiction. Where personal data leaves the EEA or UK, transfers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or an adequacy decision.

6. Security​

We apply technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk:

  • Encryption at rest — sensitive stored values (for example API credentials and integration secrets) are encrypted with Fernet (AES-128-CBC with HMAC-SHA256 authentication).
  • Webhook authentication — inbound GitHub and Stripe webhooks are verified with HMAC SHA-256 signatures, with timestamp tolerance to prevent replay.
  • Authentication — session tokens are signed JWTs issued by Clerk and validated on every request, with strict key verification.
  • Access control — API keys and rate limiting protect the analysis endpoints; access is scoped to the owning account.
  • Transport — all client traffic is served over TLS.
  • Audit trail — SHA-256 hashes make analysed material tamper-evident without requiring us to keep the raw content.
  • Observability — traces, metrics and structured logs support detection and response to incidents.

6.1. No system is perfectly secure. If you discover a vulnerability, please report it responsibly per SECURITY.md rather than disclosing it publicly.

6.2. We notify affected users and relevant authorities of a personal data breach where the law requires it.
