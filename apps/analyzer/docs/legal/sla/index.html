Service Level Agreement

Effective date: October 18, 2026 Provider: dvddv9 (Individual Entrepreneur) ("Vishap") Customer: [Customer Legal Name] Contact: dvddvd9h@outlook.com

This Service Level Agreement ("SLA") describes the availability commitment, support response times and service credits applicable to the Vishap hosted service. It supplements the Terms of Service; where this SLA and the Terms conflict on service levels, this SLA prevails.

1. Service Commitment​

1.1. Vishap targets 99.9% monthly uptime for the analysis API and the web application, measured per calendar month.

1.2. Monthly uptime is calculated as:

uptime % = (total minutes in month - excluded minutes - unavailable minutes)
           / (total minutes in month - excluded minutes) x 100

1.3. Unavailable means a request to the health endpoint (GET /health) fails, returns a 5xx response, or exceeds a 30-second response time, for two or more consecutive probe intervals from at least two independent monitoring locations.

1.4. Measurement. Availability is measured by the Prometheus metrics and Grafana recording rules operated by Vishap (vishap_http_requests_total and the synthetic health probe). Measurement data is retained for 30 days and is the authoritative record for credit calculations.

1.5. 99.9% monthly uptime corresponds to approximately 43 minutes of permitted unavailability in a 30-day month, excluding the events in Section 2.

2. Exclusions​

The following do not count as unavailability and are excluded from the uptime calculation:

2.1. Scheduled maintenance. Announced at least 48 hours in advance, within the window described in Section 6.

2.2. Emergency maintenance. Security patches or fixes required to prevent imminent harm, announced as soon as practicable; Vishap uses reasonable efforts to keep such windows under 60 minutes per month.

2.3. Force majeure. Events beyond reasonable control, including natural disasters, war, civil unrest, widespread internet or power failures, and government action.

2.4. Customer-side causes. Invalid payloads exceeding documented limits, revoked or expired API keys and JWTs, exceeding rate limits, customer network or DNS problems, or customer infrastructure.

2.5. Third-party dependencies. Outages of Clerk, Stripe, GitHub, or an LLM inference provider where the failure originates in that provider and Vishap has no reasonable workaround (for example, an automatic fallback to a local Ollama model being unavailable as well).

2.6. Beta and preview features. Features explicitly labelled beta or preview are provided without an availability commitment.

2.7. Suspension for cause. Service suspension under the Terms of Service for non-payment or breach of acceptable use.

3. Support Response Times​

3.1. Support is available by email at dvddvd9h@outlook.com, Monday to Friday, 09:00–18:00 in the Provider's local time zone, excluding public holidays.

3.2. Response time means the time from ticket receipt to a substantive human response — not to resolution.

SeverityDescriptionResponse Time
P1 (Critical)Service unavailable, or a security incident1 hour
P2 (High)Major feature broken, no workaround; analyses fail for all users4 hours
P3 (Medium)Feature degraded, workaround available; isolated job failures1 business day
P4 (Low)Cosmetic issues, questions, documentation requests3 business days

3.3. P1 issues may be reported outside business hours and are triaged on call, but response within 1 hour applies only during business hours as defined in 3.1 unless the Customer has a written 24x7 support agreement.

3.4. The Customer must provide a reproducible description, affected job or session identifiers, and timestamps. Vishap may reclassify severity in mutual agreement with the Customer.

4. Service Credits​

4.1. If monthly uptime falls below 99.9%, the Customer is entitled to a credit against the monthly fee for the affected month:

Monthly uptimeService credit
99.0% – 99.9%10% of the monthly fee
95.0% – 99.0%25% of the monthly fee
Below 95.0%50% of the monthly fee

4.2. Calculation basis. Credits are calculated on the recurring monthly fee actually paid for the affected month, excluding usage-based charges, taxes and third-party pass-through costs.

4.3. Cap. Total credits for any single month will not exceed 30% of that month's recurring fee.

4.4. Claim procedure. The Customer must request a credit within 30 days after the end of the affected month, providing the observed outage window and any supporting evidence. Vishap verifies the claim against its monitoring data and issues credits within 30 days.

4.5. Sole remedy. Service credits are the Customer's sole and exclusive remedy for failure to meet the availability commitment, except where law provides otherwise.

4.6. Credits are applied to future invoices and are not paid out in cash. Credits are not granted for accounts suspended for cause.

5. Monitoring and Reporting​

5.1. Shared dashboard. Enterprise customers receive access to a read-only Grafana dashboard showing availability, request rate, error rate, job throughput and pipeline duration.

5.2. Monthly report. On request, Vishap provides a monthly availability report containing uptime percentage, excluded windows, and a list of incidents with durations and root-cause summaries.

5.3. Status page. Planned maintenance and incidents are announced on the public status page.

5.4. Data minimisation. Dashboards and reports expose aggregate operational metrics only; they never contain other customers' data or analysed material.

6. Maintenance Windows​

6.1. Standard window. The first Sunday of each calendar month, 02:00–04:00 UTC.

6.2. Notice. Scheduled maintenance is announced at least 48 hours in advance by email and on the status page, with the expected duration and impact.

6.3. Overrun. If maintenance overruns its announced window, the excess time counts as unavailability and is not excluded, unless caused by an event in Section 2.

6.4. Emergency work. Security patches may be applied outside the standard window to address an actively exploited vulnerability; notice is given as soon as practicable.

7. Term and Termination​

7.1. This SLA applies for the term of the Customer's subscription.

7.2. Either party may terminate the SLA or the underlying subscription with 30 days' written notice, effective at the end of the then-current billing period.

7.3. Vishap may terminate this SLA immediately if the Customer breaches the acceptable use provisions of the Terms of Service.

7.4. Effect of termination. Credits accrued before termination are applied to the final invoice. Sections 4.5 (sole remedy), 8 (contact) and any provisions that by their nature survive, continue to apply.

7.5. Vishap may revise this SLA with 30 days' notice; changes apply from the next renewal period. Continued use constitutes acceptance.

8. Contact​

  • Support and P1 escalation: dvddvd9h@outlook.com
  • Security incidents: see SECURITY.md in the project repository
  • Billing and credits: billing@vishap.dev
  • Postal address: Remote — Yerevan, Armenia
