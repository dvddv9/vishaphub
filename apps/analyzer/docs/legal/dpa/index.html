Data Processing Agreement

Effective date: October 18, 2026 Controller (Customer): [Customer Legal Name] Processor: dvddv9 (Individual Entrepreneur) ("Vishap") Contact: dvddvd9h@outlook.com

This Data Processing Agreement ("DPA") forms part of the agreement between the Customer and Vishap for the use of the Vishap service and applies to the processing of Personal Data that Vishap carries out on the Customer's behalf. It supplements, and does not replace, the Terms of Service and the Privacy Policy.

1. Definitions​

1.1. "Controller" means the entity that determines the purposes and means of the processing of Personal Data — for the purposes of this DPA, the Customer.

1.2. "Processor" means the entity that processes Personal Data on behalf of the Controller — for the purposes of this DPA, Vishap.

1.3. "Data Subject" means an identified or identifiable natural person to whom Personal Data relates.

1.4. "Personal Data" means any information relating to a Data Subject as defined in Article 4(1) GDPR, contained in the material the Customer submits for analysis or generated by the Service.

1.5. "Processing" means any operation performed on Personal Data, including collection, storage, analysis, transmission and deletion.

1.6. "Sub-processor" means a third party engaged by Vishap to process Personal Data on the Customer's behalf (Section 4).

1.7. "Customer Data" means the material submitted for analysis, the generated reports, and associated metadata.

1.8. "SCCs" means the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by European Commission Decision 2021/914.

1.9. "Services" means the Vishap web application, API and command-line interface described in the Terms of Service.

2. Scope and Purpose​

2.1. Vishap processes Customer Data only to provide the Services: running the six-stage analysis pipeline, returning reports, authenticating users and administering subscriptions.

2.2. The categories of Personal Data and the purposes of processing are:

CategoryExamplesPurpose
Account dataEmail, name, profile image, organisation membershipAuthentication (Clerk), support
Submitted materialSource code, configuration, specifications, logs, dataSecurity analysis and report generation
Derived dataAudit trail (SHA-256 hashes, PII flags), token counts, costTraceability, billing

Billing dataCustomer identifier, subscription status, metered usagePayments (Stripe)

2.3. Special categories. The Services are not intended for material containing special categories of Personal Data (Article 9 GDPR) or data relating to criminal convictions. The Customer must not submit such material unless it has a documented lawful basis and has notified Vishap in writing.

2.4. Duration. Processing continues for the term of the agreement and until deletion under Section 5.

2.5. The Customer's documented instructions are this DPA, the Terms of Service, the Privacy Policy, and any written order form. Vishap will inform the Customer promptly if, in its opinion, an instruction infringes GDPR.

3. Obligations of the Processor​

3.1. Instructions. Vishap processes Personal Data only on documented instructions from the Customer, including with regard to transfers to third countries, unless required to do otherwise by Union or Member State law — in which case Vishap informs the Customer unless the law prohibits it.

3.2. Confidentiality. Personnel authorised to process Personal Data are bound by written confidentiality obligations and receive access only on a need-to-know basis.

3.3. Security. Vishap implements the technical and organisational measures in Section 6 to ensure an appropriate level of security under Article 32 GDPR.

3.4. Assistance with Data Subject requests. Taking into account the nature of the processing, Vishap assists the Customer in fulfilling its obligation to respond to requests exercising Data Subject rights (Section 8).

3.5. Breach notification. Vishap notifies the Customer without undue delay, and in any event within 72 hours of becoming aware of a Personal Data breach affecting Customer Data. The notification describes the nature of the breach, the affected data, likely consequences and measures taken or proposed. Where the notification cannot be made within 72 hours, it is made in phases with reasons for the delay.

3.6. DPIA and prior consultation. Vishap provides reasonable assistance where the Customer carries out a data protection impact assessment or prior consultation with a supervisory authority, insofar as the processing by Vishap is relevant.

3.7. Deletion or return on termination. At the Customer's choice, Vishap deletes or returns Customer Data after the end of the provision of the Services, and deletes existing copies, except where retention is required by law. Billing records are retained for the statutory period (Section 5).

3.8. Demonstrating compliance. Vishap makes available the information necessary to demonstrate compliance with Article 28 GDPR and allows audits under Section 9.

3.9. Records. Vishap maintains records of processing activities as required by Article 30 GDPR.

4.1. The Customer gives general written authorisation for Vishap to engage the sub-processors listed below.

4.2. Opting out of third-party LLM providers. The Customer may require that the analysed material is processed exclusively by a self-hosted Ollama model. In that deployment, no material is sent to a third-party LLM provider and the international transfer described in Section 7 does not occur for that data.

4.3. Change of sub-processors. Vishap gives the Customer at least 30 days' prior notice of the addition or replacement of a sub-processor, during which the Customer may object on reasonable data protection grounds. If the parties cannot resolve the objection, the Customer may terminate the affected Services without penalty.

4.4. Flow-down. Vishap imposes data protection obligations on each sub-processor that are substantially the same as, and no less protective than, those in this DPA, and remains fully liable to the Customer for their performance.

4.5. PII redaction. Personal data detected in the submitted material is redacted before transmission to an LLM provider. Redaction reduces, but does not eliminate, the risk of disclosure; the Customer should not rely on it for regulated data.

5. Data Retention​

5.1. Vishap retains Customer Data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this DPA:

5.2. Deleted data is removed from primary storage promptly; residual copies may persist in backups for up to 30 days.

5.3. Vishap may retain data longer where required by law or to resolve disputes, and will inform the Customer of any such legal retention obligation where permitted.

6. Security Measures​

6.1. Encryption at rest. Sensitive stored values — API credentials and integration secrets — are encrypted with Fernet (AES-128-CBC with HMAC-SHA256 authentication).

6.2. Webhook authentication. Inbound GitHub and Stripe webhooks are verified with HMAC SHA-256 signatures and a timestamp tolerance to prevent replay.

6.3. Authentication and access control. Sessions are signed JWTs issued by Clerk and validated on every request against published keys. The analysis API is protected by API keys and rate limiting.

6.4. Multi-tenant isolation. Job records are isolated with PostgreSQL row-level security: each request binds a tenant, and policies restrict SELECT, INSERT, UPDATE and DELETE to rows belonging to that tenant.

6.5. Audit trail. SHA-256 hashes of the analysed material make reports tamper-evident without requiring retention of raw content.

6.6. Observability. OpenTelemetry traces and Prometheus metrics record job identifiers and timings; they do not contain the analysed material or file names.

6.7. Transport encryption. All client traffic is served over TLS.

6.8. Personnel and processes. Access is granted on a least-privilege basis, reviewed periodically; a documented incident-response procedure is maintained and tested.

7. International Transfers​

7.1. Where Personal Data is transferred outside the EEA or the United Kingdom, the transfer relies on the SCCs (Module Two: controller to processor), together with the UK International Data Transfer Addendum where applicable.

7.2. Vishap has completed a transfer impact assessment for each sub-processor listed in Section 4 and will provide a summary to the Customer on request.

7.3. Escalation. Where a sub-processor is subject to government access requests that would conflict with the SCCs, Vishap will notify the Customer, and the Customer may require the opt-out in Section 4.2 or terminate the affected Services.

7.4. Vishap does not guarantee data residency in a specific jurisdiction unless recorded in a written order form.

8. Data Subject Rights​

8.1. Vishap assists the Customer in responding to Data Subject requests for access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection.

8.2. Self-service endpoints — not yet implemented (TODO). GET /api/me/data (export) and DELETE /api/me/delete (erasure) are planned but not available as of this document's effective date. Until they ship, requests are handled manually.

8.3. Manual procedure. A Data Subject, or the Customer on their behalf, emails dvddvd9h@outlook.com from the address associated with the account. Vishap verifies identity, acts within 30 days, and confirms completion in writing. The Customer may also use the analyst dashboard to locate and delete individual job records.

8.4. Where a request concerns data Vishap processes as a controller (for example account data, or email sent to support), Vishap handles it directly under the Privacy Policy and informs the Customer if the request also concerns Customer Data.

9. Audit and Inspection​

9.1. Audit right. Once per calendar year, on at least 30 days' written notice, the Customer (or an independent auditor bound by confidentiality) may audit Vishap's compliance with this DPA.

9.2. Scope and method. Audits are conducted remotely, during normal business hours, by reviewing documentation, security questionnaires and, where necessary, virtual walk-throughs. On-site inspections require mutual written agreement.

9.3. Costs. Each party bears its own costs. If the audit reveals a material breach of this DPA, Vishap reimburses the Customer's reasonable audit costs.

9.4. Limits. The Customer may not audit on-site more than once per year, access the data of other customers, or perform penetration testing without prior written consent.

9.5. Alternative. Vishap may satisfy this Section by providing a current independent security certification or a completed industry-standard questionnaire (ISO 27001, SOC 2 or equivalent), where available.

10. Liability and Indemnification​

10.1. Each party is liable to the other for damage caused by a breach of this DPA, in accordance with the liability provisions of the Terms of Service, which apply to this DPA as if set out here.

10.2. Vishap is liable for the acts and omissions of its sub-processors as if they were its own.

10.3. Indemnification. Vishap indemnifies the Customer against reasonable costs of a supervisory-authority fine imposed on the Customer as a direct result of Vishap's breach of this DPA, to the extent the fine is attributable to Vishap's failure and is not caused or contributed to by the Customer.

10.4. Exclusions. Neither party is liable under this DPA for losses caused by the other party's instructions, the Customer's unlawful submission of Personal Data, or the Customer's failure to comply with its own controller obligations.

10.5. Cap. The aggregate liability of either party under this DPA is subject to the limitation of liability in the Terms of Service. Nothing in this DPA excludes liability that cannot be excluded by law.

10.6. Precedence. In case of conflict between this DPA and the Terms of Service regarding the processing of Personal Data, this DPA prevails.
